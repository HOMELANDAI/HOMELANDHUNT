# Prize, Loyalty & Event System

## Source-Derived Tournament Structure

The current Homeland Hunt prize source specifies:

- 12 monthly contests.
- Tiered buy-ins of **$19.99 / $29.99 / $49.99**.
- Tiered monthly prize distribution.
- Cash winners plus consolation giveaways/promotions and timed free media access.
- A concept described as **"2 hours prior return on buy in"**; the source does not define its exact operational rule, so implementation must not assume one without clarification.
- Leaderboard status determining the amount/eligibility for a grand-prize game identified with **Mixcast View**.
- Special February and December contests with more winners (two) and sponsorship potential.
- Yearly Homeland Retreats.
- Special early-bird Legacy Member price: **$99.99**.
- Possible creative Legacy bonuses.

### Grand-Prize Eligibility

- **Level 1:** must enter 10 of 12 contests.
- **Level 2:** must enter 7 of 12 contests.
- **Level 3:** must enter 4 of 12 contests.

Tiebreakers can be broken by loyalty rewards or leaderboard position.

## Prize Distribution Models Under Consideration

The source discusses four approaches:

1. Percentage-based tiers.
2. Sliding scale.
3. Performance-based tiers.
4. Hybrid distribution combining ranking with performance bonuses.

The source also contains example purse distributions for a $1M purse/25 winners and a $250K purse/10 winners. Those examples should be treated as exploratory models rather than final Homeland Hunt payout commitments.

## Recommended Product Interpretation

Use a hybrid model:

- transparent placement payouts;
- limited performance bonuses;
- non-cash consolation rewards;
- NFT/collectible prizes where appropriate;
- loyalty/Legacy recognition;
- sponsor-funded special categories.

This preserves clarity while creating more moments worth watching.

## February / December Event Spikes

These events should function as intentional FOMO peaks:

- higher payout potential;
- two or more headline winners where funded;
- sponsor integration;
- Legacy-member eligibility/perks;
- alternatively, higher-than-normal event entry for eligible non-Legacy players;
- limited HAI collectible/tool releases;
- premium broadcast treatment.

## Compliance Note

Cash-prize entry systems, paid contests, NFT assets and transferable economic rewards can trigger jurisdiction-specific gaming, sweepstakes, contest, tax, consumer-protection and securities questions. Before launch, event rules, eligibility, geographic restrictions, age requirements, prize funding, NFT utility and secondary-market representations should receive qualified legal review.
