# XR / AR / Spatial AI Roadmap

## Relevance to Homeland Hunt

AR/XR glasses are highly relevant but should be an expansion path rather than an MVP dependency.

NVIDIA XR AI-style systems can connect video, audio, depth, pose and sensor inputs to multimodal agents, retrieval, reasoning and tools. That aligns with Homeland Hunt's need for characters and companions that understand spatial context.

NVIDIA ACE-style co-playable characters are similarly relevant to the game's goal of making AI characters active participants rather than static dialogue terminals.

## Spatial Homeland Hunt

Future experiences can include:

- clues anchored to real-world locations;
- virtual artist/NPC encounters;
- hands-free lyric analysis;
- spatial navigation arrows;
- object activation through gaze/voice;
- team hunts at resorts, festivals, ports or tourism districts;
- real-world Easter eggs;
- location-specific HAI experiences.

## Example Spatial Flow

1. Player arrives at an HAI Hunt zone.
2. Glasses authenticate the player/session.
3. Spatial map identifies permitted hunt surfaces/anchors.
4. Artist/guide agent appears or communicates through audio.
5. Player discovers a lyric clue attached to a location.
6. Helmet analyzes permitted clue context.
7. Anchor stores a checkpoint.
8. Timepiece monitors remaining challenge window.
9. Shield manages defined defensive mechanics.
10. Server validates completion and updates leaderboard.

## Real-World Expansion

Dominican Republic tourism/event deployments could become a differentiated extension: resorts, festivals, cultural districts and excursions can host temporary Hunt zones while the same account, inventory, Legacy status and reward system persists across digital and physical experiences.

## Implementation Principle

Do not require glasses for basic Homeland Hunt participation. Build a platform-neutral game-state API first, then attach PC/mobile/console/XR clients to the same authoritative backend.
